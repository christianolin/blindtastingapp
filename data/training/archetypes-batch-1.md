## Notes for review

- **Count.** §4.7 names 82 wines (its "Rest of France (10)" heading lists 12). Its five pairs are split into one archetype each so a bottle of either earns appellation points, giving 87.
- **The five pairs, split:** Puligny-Montrachet and Chassagne-Montrachet share every range and aroma and differ in appellation and description. Chablis Grand Cru sits a step above Premier Cru in intensity, body and finish (and in typical age and quality range), each with only its own designation. Hermitage is fuller, longer and older-drinking than Crozes-Hermitage, with a higher quality range. Fino is Jerez / Jerez-Xérès-Sherry with the Fino designation; Manzanilla is Andalucia / Manzanilla Sanlucar de Barrameda DO with the Manzanilla designation (the live row sits under region Andalucia, not Jerez, so the two do not share region points; the map has no Manzanilla place, so it hangs off the Andalucía region key). Clare Valley GI and Eden Valley GI share ranges; Eden Valley has a more floral, stony aroma set.
- **Generic names pinned to one appellation:** Beaujolais cru → Morgon AOC; Saint-Émilion → Saint-Émilion Grand Cru AOC; Muscadet → Muscadet Sevre et Maine AOC; Provence rosé → Côtes de Provence AOC; Primitivo → Primitivo di Manduria DOC; Etna Rosso → Etna Rosso DOC; Spätburgunder → Baden; Blaufränkisch → Mittelburgenland DAC; Sonoma Chardonnay → Sonoma Coast AVA; Chilean Cabernet → Maipo Valley DO.
- **Duplicate reference rows** (the live tables hold two spellings or two parents): Ribera del Duero DO, Priorat, Rías Baixas, Jerez, Santorini, Wachau, Stellenbosch and Port each exist twice. The batch uses the row the catalog already uses where one does (Ribera del Duero/Ribera del Duero DO, Priorat/Priorat DOQ, Porto/Porto DOC, Coastal Region/… WO), otherwise the row under the wine region's own region with the designation suffix.
- **Grapes:** Grenache (not Garnacha) and Pinot Noir (not Spätburgunder/Pinot Nero) — the spellings the catalog uses; Viura for Macabeo.
- **Designations:** Vouvray carries Sec / Demi-Sec (the table files them under Sparkling Dosage, but they are Vouvray's legal label terms).
- **Fortified alcohol** is written on the five-stop ladder as WSET grades it among fortified wines (Fino low–medium(-), Oloroso medium–medium(+), Port medium(+)–high).
