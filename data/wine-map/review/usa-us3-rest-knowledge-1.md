## United States › California › Antelope Valley of the California High Desert

Name: **Antelope Valley of the California High Desert** · key `united-states.california.antelope-valley-of-the-california-high-desert` · AVA

**Description.** A high-desert valley of about 665 square miles in Los Angeles and Kern counties, north of the San Gabriel Mountains, with a floor at 2,300 to 3,100 feet walled by mountains on three sides. Vines were planted here in the 1880s and 1890s; modern planting began in 1981, and by 2007 there were about 128 acres in 16 vineyards.

**Climate.** Hot, dry summers and cold winters, Winkler Region V at about 4,600 growing degree days, with only 4 to 9 inches of rain and winter lows of 6 to 11 °F.

**Soils.** Deep, loamy, well-drained alluvial soils from granite, such as the Hesperia-Rosamond-Cajon and Hanford-Ramona-Greenfield associations.

**Grape varieties (text).** Cabernet Sauvignon, Tempranillo and Malbec, with Muscat, on the estates of the valley.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from Cabernet Sauvignon, Tempranillo and Malbec.

**Key facts**

- Established 2011 (27 CFR 9.219)
- About 665 square miles (TTB final rule)
- About 128 acres in 16 vineyards in 2007 (TTB final rule)
- Spans Los Angeles and Kern counties

## United States › California › Central Coast › Monterey › Hames Valley

Name: **Hames Valley** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.monterey.hames-valley` · AVA

**Description.** The southernmost Monterey County AVA, a valley of about 10,240 acres near Bradley toward the San Luis Obispo county line, at about 472 to 1,289 feet. About 2,200 acres are planted, and its growers favour Rhône varieties as well as Bordeaux ones.

## United States › California › Central Coast › Monterey › San Bernabe

Name: **San Bernabe** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.monterey.san-bernabe` · AVA

**Description.** An AVA of about 24,796 acres of rolling foothills and valley floor in the Salinas Valley south of King City, about 9 miles long and 7 miles wide, within a single large vineyard estate. At establishment about 7,636 acres were planted, all below 700 feet.

**Climate.** About 13 inches of rain a year and around 30 frost days; marine wind from Monterey Bay moderates the heat, and the average daily swing is about 30 °F.

**Grape varieties (text).** Merlot, Syrah, Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Riesling among others.

## United States › California › Central Coast › San Antonio Valley

Name: **San Antonio Valley** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.san-antonio-valley` · AVA

**Description.** A basin of about 150,400 acres in southwestern Monterey County, the watershed of the San Antonio River, encircled by the Santa Lucia Range between the Pacific and the Salinas Valley. It is named after Mission San Antonio de Padua, founded in 1771. Over 700 acres were planted at establishment.

**Climate.** Shut off from marine air by the mountains: drier, hotter summer days and cooler nights, over 3,000 degree days, and wider seasonal swings than its neighbours.

**Soils.** Around 40 soil series, mostly alluvial on flat to gentle slopes, including Arbuckle gravelly loam, Lockwood loam and Placentia sandy loam.

**Grape varieties (text).** Cabernet Sauvignon, Petite Sirah and Syrah, with Grenache, Tempranillo and Marsanne among some 20 varieties.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from Cabernet Sauvignon, Petite Sirah and Rhône varieties.

**Key facts**

- Established 2006 (27 CFR 9.194)
- About 150,400 acres; elevations from about 850 to 2,530 feet (T.D. TTB-46)
- Named after Mission San Antonio de Padua, founded in 1771 (T.D. TTB-46)

## United States › California › Central Coast › San Benito

Name: **San Benito** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.san-benito` · AVA

**Description.** An AVA of about 45,000 acres in San Benito County, south of Hollister, between the Gabilan and Diablo ranges. It holds Cienega Valley, Lime Kiln Valley and Paicines, and some of the oldest vineyards in California; Almaden farmed much of it from the 1950s.

**Climate.** Moderate, with a maritime influence from Monterey Bay, about 20 miles away, and afternoon breezes; the mountain ranges shelter the valleys.

**Soils.** Varied, because of seismic activity: soils shift from granite to limestone within feet, with ancient seabed deposits.

**Grape varieties (text).** Pinot Noir and Chardonnay make up more than half the county's vineyard; Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Zinfandel grow in the warmer spots.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and red wines from older Zinfandel plantings.

**Key facts**

- Established 1987 (27 CFR 9.110)
- About 45,000 acres at establishment (Wine Institute, from the TTB final rule)
- Contains Cienega Valley, Lime Kiln Valley and Paicines

## United States › California › Central Coast › San Benito › Cienega Valley

Name: **Cienega Valley** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.san-benito.cienega-valley` · AVA

**Description.** A valley south of Hollister in western San Benito County, below the Gabilan Range, with Pescadero Creek running through its vineyards and the San Andreas fault dividing its floor. It holds some of the oldest continuously producing vineyards in California, including head-trained Zinfandel from 1906.

**Climate.** Moderate, sheltered by the mountains and cooled by Monterey Bay, about 20 miles away, with a daily afternoon breeze in summer.

**Soils.** Granite and limestone on the western side of the fault, granite and sandstone on the eastern side.

**Grape varieties (text).** Old-vine Zinfandel and Pinot Noir, with Chardonnay.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from old-vine Zinfandel and Pinot Noir, and dry Chardonnay.

**Key facts**

- Established 1982 (27 CFR 9.38)
- The San Andreas fault divides the valley floor (Please the Palate)

## United States › California › Central Coast › San Francisco Bay › Contra Costa

Name: **Contra Costa** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.san-francisco-bay.contra-costa` · AVA

**Description.** An AVA of about 167,146 acres covering the flat and gently rolling north and east of Contra Costa County, around Oakley, Brentwood and Antioch, mostly below 100 feet. At establishment it had at least 60 vineyards on about 1,700 acres and 14 wineries.

**Grape varieties (text).** Zinfandel, Petite Sirah and Mourvèdre, with Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay.

Name: **Santa Clara Valley** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.san-francisco-bay.santa-clara-valley` · AVA

## United States › California › Dos Rios

Name: **Dos Rios** · key `united-states.california.dos-rios` · AVA

**Description.** A canyon of about 15,500 acres in northern Mendocino County where the Eel River meets its Middle Fork, about 12 miles long and 4 wide. "Dos Rios" is Spanish for "two rivers". Only about six acres of vines were planted at establishment.

**Climate.** Average temperatures of 52 to 58 °F and 30 to 60 inches of rain, mostly October to April; marine winds moderate the heat and the rivers reflect extra sunlight into the canyon.

**Soils.** Well-drained to excessively drained loams, sandy loams and gravelly loams from sandstone and siltstone, on canyon walls sloping 30 to 75 percent.

**Grape varieties (text).** Zinfandel, with Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Chardonnay.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from a few small vineyards.

**Key facts**

- Established 2005 (27 CFR 9.175)
- About 15,500 acres; about six acres planted at establishment (TTB final rule)
- Its upper edge is the 2,000-foot contour line (TTB final rule)

- Cabernet Sauvignon · accessory
- Chardonnay · accessory
- Merlot · accessory

**Styles:** Red

## United States › California › Inwood Valley

Name: **Inwood Valley** · key `united-states.california.inwood-valley` · AVA

**Description.** An AVA of about 28,441 acres in the foothills east of Redding in Shasta County, below Blue Mountain, covering land between 1,000 and 3,000 feet. At establishment five vineyards covered about 62.5 acres. Merlot was planted at the Inwood Ranch in the late 1970s, and wild Mission vines survive from plantings of 1864.

**Climate.** Regions II to III, about 2,700 to 3,400 growing degree days, with about 57 inches of rain a year, 14 of them in the growing season.

**Soils.** Some 27 soil series, the Aiken series nearly a quarter of the area.

**Grape varieties (text).** Merlot is the variety the rulemaking records, first planted at the Inwood Ranch in the late 1970s.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from a few small foothill vineyards.

**Key facts**

- Established 2012 (27 CFR 9.226)
- About 28,441 acres; five vineyards on about 62.5 acres at establishment (TTB final rule)
- Only land between 1,000 and 3,000 feet is included (TTB proposed rule, 2011)

**Grapes**, as the details panel lists them (signature grapes first, then the ones it tags "accessory"; each group alphabetically):

- Merlot

**Styles:** Red
