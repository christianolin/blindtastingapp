**Description.** An AVA of about 44,590 acres in the Santa Monica Mountains of Los Angeles and Ventura counties, above the Malibu coast. At establishment 52 small vineyards covered about 198 acres. It holds Malibu-Newton Canyon and Saddle Rock-Malibu.

## United States › California › North Coast › Benmore Valley

Name: **Benmore Valley** · key `united-states.california.north-coast.benmore-valley` · AVA

**Description.** A small, high valley of about 1,440 acres in southwestern Lake County, at about 2,400 feet among mountains averaging 2,800 feet. Its only commercial vineyard was the Trione family's Chardonnay, grown for Geyser Peak; the harsh climate defeated it, most vines were removed, and the valley is now mainly grazing land. It is named after Benjamin Logan Moore, a 19th-century cattle rustler.

**Climate.** Cold for California: the growing season starts late, the average last frost comes in late May, and frost has been recorded as late as the Fourth of July.

**Soils.** Soils of an enclosed mountain valley floor and its surrounding slopes.

**Grape varieties (text).** Chardonnay was the only grape grown commercially here.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry white wine from Chardonnay, when the vineyard was farmed.

**Key facts**

- Established 1991 (27 CFR 9.138)
- About 1,440 acres at about 2,400 feet (Lake County News)
- Named after Benjamin Logan Moore (Lake County News)

## United States › California › North Coast › High Valley

Name: **High Valley** · key `united-states.california.north-coast.high-valley` · AVA

**Description.** An enclosed, bowl-shaped valley of about 14,000 acres above the eastern shore of Clear Lake near Clearlake Oaks, running east to west, which is unusual in the Coast Ranges. ## United States › California › North Coast › Mendocino › McDowell Valley

Name: **McDowell Valley** · key `united-states.california.north-coast.mendocino.mcdowell-valley` · AVA

**Description.** A small valley east of Hopland in Mendocino County, ringed by mountains, known for some of the oldest Syrah and Grenache in California. The first varietal Syrah released in California came from these vines, from the 1978 vintage. Cold air gathers on the valley floor at night.

**Climate.** Warm days and cool nights: cold air collects on the enclosed valley floor after dark, which keeps acidity in the grapes.

**Soils.** Soils of the valley floor and benches, dry-farmed in the old blocks.

**Grape varieties (text).** Syrah and Grenache, with Petite Sirah, in old head-trained, dry-farmed blocks.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from Syrah and Grenache, and dry rosé.

**Key facts**

- Established 1981 (27 CFR 9.36)
- California's first varietal Syrah came from here, the 1978 vintage (Historic Vineyard Society)
- Lies within the Mendocino AVA

## United States › California › Yucaipa Valley

Name: **Yucaipa Valley** · key `united-states.california.yucaipa-valley` · AVA

**Description.** An AVA of about 36,467 acres of rolling foothills of the San Bernardino Mountains in San Bernardino County, taking in Yucaipa, Calimesa and Oak Glen, from 2,000 to 4,600 feet. At establishment it had about 23 vineyards and two wineries.

**Climate.** Hot and dry: average temperatures in Yucaipa run from about 49 to 78 °F, with August averaging 97 °F, and only about 4 inches of rain in the growing season; the height concentrates sunlight.

**Soils.** Soils of rolling foothill slopes below the San Bernardino Mountains.

**Grape varieties (text).** Cabernet Sauvignon, Zinfandel and Syrah, with Merlot, Malbec, Nebbiolo, Barbera and Petite Sirah.
