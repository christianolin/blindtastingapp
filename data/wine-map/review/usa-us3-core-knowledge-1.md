## United States › California › Central Coast › Alisos Canyon

Name: **Alisos Canyon** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.alisos-canyon` · AVA

**Description.** A small AVA of about 5,774 acres near Los Alamos in northern Santa Barbara County, where the San Antonio Creek valley runs about 20 miles west to the Pacific. At establishment it had nine commercial vineyards on about 238 acres and one winery, and Syrah is its most planted grape.

**Climate.** Winkler Region II, about 25 miles from the ocean, in a transition between coast and inland: cool marine air and fog arrive along the San Antonio Creek valley.

**Soils.** Mostly sandstone and shale soils of the Paso Robles Formation (63%) and Careaga Sandstone (13%), sandy and quick-draining, with calcium from shale and a rare streak of limestone.

**Grape varieties (text).** Syrah first, with Grenache, Viognier and Cabernet Franc.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines, mainly Syrah and Grenache, and some Viognier.

**Key facts**

- Established 2020 (27 CFR 9.270)
- About 5,774 acres (TTB final rule)
- Nine vineyards on about 238 acres at establishment (TTB final rule)

## United States › California › Central Coast › Gabilan Mountains

Name: **Gabilan Mountains** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.gabilan-mountains` · AVA

**Description.** A mountain AVA of about 98,000 acres in the Gabilan Range, across the Monterey–San Benito county line east of the Salinas Valley. It holds the smaller Chalone and Mt. Harlan AVAs. At establishment it had six commercial vineyards, about 436 acres, and four wineries.

**Climate.** High and dry: the area averages about 2,370 feet, has fewer than 2.5 hours of fog or cloud a day from June to September (7 to 9 in the areas around it) and about 17 inches of rain a year.

**Soils.** Moderately coarse soils over granite bedrock, mainly of the Sheridan-Cieneba-Auberry association, rich in calcium from limestone and quick to drain.

**Grape varieties (text).** Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, the grapes of Chalone and Mt. Harlan, which it contains.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry Pinot Noir and Chardonnay from high, limestone-rich sites.

**Key facts**

- About 98,000 acres in Monterey and San Benito counties (TTB final rule)
- Six vineyards, about 436 acres, and four wineries at establishment (TTB final rule)

## United States › California › Central Coast › Monterey

Name: **Monterey** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.monterey` · AVA

**Description.** A large AVA that runs almost the whole length of Monterey County, mostly along the Salinas Valley from Monterey Bay south toward the San Luis Obispo County line. It holds Arroyo Seco, Santa Lucia Highlands, Hames Valley, San Bernabe and San Lucas. About 40,000 acres are planted, and Chardonnay is more than half of them.

**Climate.** Cool to hot along its roughly 100-mile length: wind and fog from Monterey Bay funnel up the Salinas Valley in the afternoon, so the north is cool and the south warm, with a long growing season and little rain.

**Soils.** Loams and sandy loams on the valley floor and benches, from the Chualar and Garey series to Lockwood shaly loam in the south.

**Grape varieties (text).** Chardonnay over half the vineyard, with Pinot Noir, Riesling, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah and Sauvignon Blanc.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and red wines from the warmer south.

**Key facts**

- Established 1984 (27 CFR 9.98)
- About 40,000 acres planted; Chardonnay over 50% (Monterey County Vintners & Growers)
- Elevations from sea level to about 2,320 feet (Monterey County Vintners & Growers)

## United States › California › Central Coast › Paso Robles

Name: **Paso Robles** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.paso-robles` · AVA

**Description.** A large inland AVA in northern San Luis Obispo County, about 614,000 acres east of the Santa Lucia Range, with more than 40,000 acres of vines and over 200 wineries. It is split by the Salinas River into a cooler, hillier west side and warmer rolling plains in the east. In 2014 eleven smaller AVAs were established within it.

**Climate.** Warm, dry days and cool nights, with some of the widest day-to-night swings in California; marine air reaches the west through the Templeton Gap and other passes in the Santa Lucia Range, while the east is warmer and more continental.

**Soils.** Very varied: calcareous shale and limestone-derived soils on the western hills, and alluvial terraces and fans of the Salinas and Estrella rivers in the east.

**Grape varieties (text).** Cabernet Sauvignon is about 39% of the vineyard, then Merlot, Syrah and Zinfandel, with Grenache, Mourvèdre, Petite Sirah and Viognier among many others.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines from Cabernet Sauvignon, Zinfandel and Rhône varieties, Rhône-style whites and dry rosé.

**Key facts**

- Established 1983 (27 CFR 9.84)
- About 614,000 acres with more than 40,000 acres of vines (Paso Robles Wine Country Alliance)
- Eleven AVAs were established within it in 2014 (T.D. TTB-125)
- Planted: Cabernet Sauvignon 39%, Merlot 14%, Syrah 9%, Zinfandel 8% (Paso Robles Wine Country Alliance)

Name: **Adelaida District** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.paso-robles.adelaida-district` · AVA

## United States › California › Central Coast › Paso Robles › Paso Robles Geneseo District

Name: **Paso Robles Geneseo District** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.paso-robles.paso-robles-geneseo-district` · AVA

**Description.** A district of about 17,300 acres east of the town of Paso Robles, on upfaulted hills and old river terraces along the Huerhuero–La Panza fault, from 740 to 1,300 feet. German settlers founded the Geneseo community in the 1880s, and the Ernst family planted vines in 1884; some of Paso Robles's oldest Syrah grows here.

**Climate.** Cool, Region I: fog comes in off the ocean most mornings and afternoons, the wind is steady, and summer days stay around the mid-70s °F, giving one of the longest growing seasons in California.

**Soils.** Sandy and loamy soils on the valley floor and benches, well drained.

**Grape varieties (text).** Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, with Syrah as the third grape.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, and cool-climate Syrah.

**Key facts**

- Established 1981 (27 CFR 9.28)
- California's second AVA (Santa Barbara Vintners)
- Spans Santa Barbara and San Luis Obispo counties
- About 7,500 acres of vineyards (Santa Maria Valley Wine Country)

## United States › California › Central Coast › Santa Ynez Valley

Name: **Santa Ynez Valley** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.santa-ynez-valley` · AVA

**Description.** A long east–west valley in Santa Barbara County, the Santa Ynez River's, that opens to the Pacific at Lompoc and runs inland past Buellton, Solvang and Los Olivos toward Lake Cachuma. Temperatures rise by about a degree for every mile from the sea, so it grows Pinot Noir and Chardonnay in the west, Rhône varieties in the middle and Bordeaux varieties in the east. It holds Sta. Rita Hills, Ballard Canyon, Los Olivos District and Happy Canyon of Santa Barbara.

**Climate.** Cool in the west and warm in the east: fog settles on the vines at night and clears by midday, and a single vineyard often sees swings of more than 50 °F between day and night.

**Soils.** Varied: marine sands and sedimentary soils in the west, alluvial terraces in the middle, and serpentine-rich soils in the far east.

**Grape varieties (text).** Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Syrah lead, with Sauvignon Blanc, Cabernet Sauvignon and Grenache.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry reds and whites from Burgundy, Rhône and Bordeaux varieties, and some dry rosé.

**Key facts**

- Established 1983 (27 CFR 9.54)
- Contains Sta. Rita Hills, Ballard Canyon, Los Olivos District and Happy Canyon of Santa Barbara
- Temperature rises about 1 °F per mile inland (Santa Barbara Vintners)
- County plantings 2023: Chardonnay 3,471 acres, Pinot Noir 2,913, Syrah 975 (Santa Barbara Vintners)

## United States › California › Central Coast › Santa Ynez Valley › Ballard Canyon

Name: **Ballard Canyon** · key `united-states.california.central-coast.santa-ynez-valley.ballard-canyon` · AVA

**Description.** A small north–south canyon of about 7,800 acres between Solvang, Buellton and Los Olivos, in the middle of the Santa Ynez Valley. About 550 acres are planted, and its growers focus on Syrah: over half the vineyard is Syrah and another 30% other Rhône varieties.

**Climate.** In between the cool west and warm east of the valley; the north–south canyon brings its own pattern of wind, fog and marine influence.

**Soils.** Soils of the canyon's slopes and floor within the Santa Ynez Valley's sedimentary hills.

**Grape varieties (text).** Syrah over half the vineyard, with Grenache, Viognier and Roussanne making up about 30% more.

**Wine styles (text).** Dry red wines, above all Syrah, and Rhône-style whites.

**Key facts**

- Established 2013 (27 CFR 9.230)
- About 7,800 acres, 550 planted (Santa Barbara Vintners)
- Over half the vineyard is Syrah (Santa Barbara Vintners)
