The aroma/flavour icons in this folder come from two sources, chosen per term for
whichever has the clearest, most literal glyph:

1. Fluent Emoji (flat) — © Microsoft, MIT licence
   https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji
2. game-icons.net — by Lorc, Delapouite and contributors, CC-BY 3.0
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
   (vendored pre-tinted; one fill colour per aroma term)

Both are fetched via the Iconify API. The term -> set/icon/colour mapping lives in
src/lib/wset/aroma-icons.mjs. Files are named by a slug derived from the term.
