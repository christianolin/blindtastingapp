The aroma/flavour icons in this folder are from game-icons.net, by Lorc,
Delapouite and other contributors, licensed CC-BY 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Each icon is vendored pre-tinted (one fill colour per aroma term) via the Iconify
API. The term -> icon + colour mapping lives in src/lib/wset/aroma-icons.mjs.
Files are named by a slug derived from the aroma term.
